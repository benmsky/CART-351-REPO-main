WEEK 1: SEPT 14th

This week was a good introduction to what CART 351 is going to be about. Looking through the first reading and lecture material helped me see web pages a bit differently. Instead of just looking at a website as a still image or layout, it is really about networks, data, and how pages connect to one another. Soon I'll be setting up this journal page as its own file and linking it back to my main index page as a simple example of that idea. It makes me think about how people actually click through and navigate a site. Since these journal posts are public and accessible online, it also makes the work feel a lot more real. It is not just sitting on my local computer, but actually living on the web where others can see it. As the semester goes on, I am looking forward to getting more comfortable with building interactive web projects and seeing how far I can push the design ideas in my work.
